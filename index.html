Illustrative client case study

The Flower Pot BK — Winter Sales Pilot

Known: The Brooklyn florist has a BloomNation storefront. The owner reports difficulty accessing a site at times and weak winter sales, and reports paying $500 monthly. Not confirmed: which BloomNation services are included, who administers every site, and the causes of either problem.

This is a proposed improvement plan, not a claim that BloomNation failed contractual duties. Review the invoice, agreement, access errors and available reports before attributing responsibility.

Keep the existing storefront

BloomNation remains the transactional channel unless the owner chooses otherwise. Verify available exports, roles and platform features first.

Add a focused follow-up dashboard

Track custom inquiries, quotes, reply dates and which inquiries become paid orders. Start with manual entry and approved exports.

Test one winter offer

January: winter gift concept. February: Valentine's preorders. March: small corporate arrangement trial. Each requires approved inventory, price and fulfillment capacity.

Report meaningful results

Inquiries, response times, quotes, paid orders, sales and estimated contribution after direct costs. Never present fabricated projections as actual sales.
